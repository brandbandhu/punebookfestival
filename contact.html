Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the festival

Is the festival free to attend?

Yes! Pune Book Festival is completely free for all attendees. Some special workshops may have limited seats and require registration.

Do I need to register in advance?

General entry is open to all. However, we recommend registering for specific workshops and author sessions to guarantee your spot.

Are there activities for children?

Absolutely! We have a dedicated Kids Corner with storytelling, puppet shows, art activities, and age-appropriate workshops.

Can I volunteer at the festival?

Yes! We welcome volunteers. Please contact us with your interests and availability, and we'll match you with suitable opportunities.

Is food available at the venue?

Yes, we have food stalls and refreshment counters throughout the festival grounds offering a variety of snacks and beverages.

How can I become a sponsor?

We offer various sponsorship packages. Please contact us at partners@punebookfestival.org for detailed information about sponsorship opportunities.
