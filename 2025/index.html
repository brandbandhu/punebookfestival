Festival recap figures

7 July 2025

The July announcement

At a Pune press conference, National Book Trust, India director Yuvraj Malik announced the third edition?s dates, venue and Joy of Reading theme.

  • Free access to more than 3,000 downloadable books was announced.
  • A mobile exhibition would bring Marathi, Hindi and English books to Pune and nearby rural communities.
  • The organisers announced awards for media coverage and photography, and outlined copyright checks for exhibitors.
