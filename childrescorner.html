Children's Corner

Where young minds discover the magic of reading and storytelling

🎪 Fun Activities for Kids

Specially designed programs to make reading fun and engaging for children

📖

Interactive Storytelling

Professional storytellers bring fairy tales, folk stories, and adventures to life with props, voices, and audience participation.

Age Group: 3-12 years
🎨

Art & Craft Workshops

Create bookmarks, design book covers, make paper puppets, and craft story-themed art projects with guidance from expert instructors.

Age Group: 5-15 years
🎭

Puppet Shows

Colorful puppet performances featuring popular children's stories, moral tales, and educational content presented in an entertaining format.

Age Group: 2-10 years
🏆

Reading Competitions

Fun reading challenges, spelling bees, and book quizzes with exciting prizes to encourage reading habits among children.

Age Group: 6-16 years
✍️

Meet Children's Authors

Special sessions with renowned children's book authors, illustrators, and creators sharing their writing journey and inspiration.

Age Group: 8-16 years
📚

Make Your Own Book

Hands-on workshop where children create their own mini books, write stories, and illustrate them with help from professional guides.

Age Group: 7-14 years
