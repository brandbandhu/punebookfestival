About Pune Book Festival

About Mr. Rajesh Pande

Mr. Rajesh Pande is the visionary Founder of Pune Book Festival, India's largest book festival. With over two decades of expertise in fostering a love for literature, he has transformed the event into a world record-breaking celebration of reading and books.

Starting in 2021 with a humble vision, Mr. Pande's leadership has propelled the festival to new heights, attracting over 10 lakh visitors and generating ₹40+ crores in book sales. His unwavering commitment to promoting literature has played a pivotal role in positioning Pune as a leading literary hub in India.

Mr. Pande's vision extends beyond just organizing a festival - he aims to create a sustainable reading ecosystem that nurtures young talent, supports emerging authors, and bridges the gap between traditional and contemporary literature.

Our Mission

Pune Bal Pustak Jatra is dedicated to celebrating literature, promoting reading culture, and creating a platform where book lovers, authors, and literary enthusiasts can come together to share their passion for the written word.

Founded with the vision of making Pune a literary hub, our festival brings together diverse voices from the world of literature. We believe that books have the power to transform lives, bridge communities, and inspire positive change in society.

Through our annual festival, we aim to nurture young talent, support emerging authors, and provide a space for meaningful literary discourse that reflects the multicultural spirit of Pune. Our mission extends to creating a sustainable reading ecosystem for future generations.

What to Expect

Discover the diverse range of activities and experiences waiting for you at the festival

🎤

Author Talks & Panels

Engage with renowned authors, poets, and literary personalities in intimate conversations about their work, writing process, and literary insights. Our panel discussions cover diverse topics from contemporary literature to classical works.

🛠️

Writing Workshops

Participate in hands-on workshops covering various aspects of creative writing, from poetry and short stories to novel writing and journalism. Learn from experienced writers and hone your craft.

👶

Children's Events

Special programming for young readers including interactive storytelling sessions, puppet shows, art and craft activities, and age-appropriate workshops designed to foster a love for reading from an early age.

📚

Book Stalls & Exhibitions

Browse through extensive collections from leading publishers, independent bookstores, and local vendors. Discover new releases, rare finds, and exclusive festival editions at special prices.

V-Blogs: Stories in Motion

Our innovative V-Blog (Video Blog) series brings literature to life through engaging video content. These short, compelling videos feature book reviews, author interviews, reading recommendations, and literary discussions.

V-Blogs serve as a bridge between traditional reading and modern digital consumption, making literature more accessible to younger audiences while maintaining the depth and richness of literary discourse.

What you'll find in our V-Blogs:

  • 📖 Book Reviews: In-depth reviews of latest releases and classics
  • 🎬 Author Spotlights: Exclusive interviews and conversations
  • 📝 Reading Lists: Curated recommendations for different genres
  • 🎭 Literary Performances: Poetry readings and dramatic interpretations
